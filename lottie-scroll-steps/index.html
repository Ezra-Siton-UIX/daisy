Return request

Customers issue a return request using your current RMA system.

AI Quality Assurance

Our AI quality assurance engine combines data and image recognition, to provide immediate feedback and ensure only eligible items will be resold.

Virtual warehouse

Returned items are listed in Uturn’s virtual warehouse where you can manage them, set reselling rules, and get insightful data from a single, intuitive dashboard.

Embedded widgets

UTurn's Smart widgets provide maximum conversion at minimum exposure. Customers can add Re: Turn items to the shopping cart to their regular Checkout!

Return shipped directly

The sold return item is sent to a local customer directly, cutting down on shipping and logistics costs. Items that aren’t sold go back to your warehouse.
